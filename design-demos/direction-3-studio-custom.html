Intent

Build the transportation, connectivity and computing systems that can extend human possibility from Earth to orbit—and, over time, beyond.

Enter the mission

01 / Mission axes

One mission.
Three operating systems.

Ambition becomes credible when each layer is named: how we move, how we connect, and what is still being developed.

01.A

Space

Reusable launch vehicles and spacecraft move payloads, cargo and people to Earth orbit and support the long path toward other worlds.

Operational + R&D
01.B

Connectivity

Starlink provides broadband internet through a constellation in low Earth orbit; availability remains subject to region and regulation.

Service
01.C

AI

In development
Designed for full + rapid reuse

Starship

Starship combines the spacecraft and Super Heavy booster in a transportation system designed for full and rapid reuse. The design intent is stated precisely; the engineering program is allowed to remain engineering.

02 / Capability path

From today’s systems to the long horizon.

Each program is indexed by role and state. The sequence separates validated capability, active service and long-term development.

02.01

Starship

A transportation system composed of Starship and Super Heavy, designed around full and rapid reuse.

02.02

Falcon

Falcon 9 is a reusable, two-stage orbital-class rocket and a present-day foundation of launch operations.

02.03

Dragon

Dragon carries people and cargo to Earth orbit and can transport up to seven passengers.

02.04

Starlink

A low-Earth-orbit satellite constellation built to provide broadband internet where service is available.

02.05

STARMIND

An orbital AI computing system currently in development. Its status remains part of its identity.

02.06

Mars & Beyond

A long-term direction for making life multiplanetary—not a countdown, a date or a promise of arrival.

03 / Computing enters orbit

IN DEVELOPMENT

STARMIND

STARMIND is described as an orbital AI computing system in development. In this information architecture, uncertainty is not hidden in fine print: development status is the primary signal.

04 / Mars & beyond / Long horizon

The mission is a direction.

Making life multiplanetary is the long view. The path is built from present systems, continued testing and work that still has to be done.
